Build a prototype web app called “Recent Movers Manufacturing Alert” that automatically finds and emails daily alerts about UK companies that have recently moved into commercial premises, with a strong focus on manufacturing. Strictly use business data from the Emailmovers API for all company discovery and enrichment (company name, contact person, phone, email, address, SIC, headcount, group/brand ownership, move-in date, premises type, and an “energy meter present” flag if available). If a sandbox is needed, mock only the transport layer but keep the data model and endpoints aligned to the Emailmovers API.

Core user story

As a sales user, I want to receive daily email notifications containing fresh leads of companies that moved into commercial premises in the last 24 hours, filtered to specific premises and business types, so I can contact the right decision makers quickly.

Filters & logic (apply server-side before any alert is sent)

Time window / freshness

Move-in date: last 1–3 days (default to last 24 hours for email alerts).

Allow manual override for “last 7 days” when browsing in the UI.

Premises / business types (INCLUDE)

Hotels, guest houses, industrial units, garage services.

Manufacturing services (highest priority).

Construction only if premises record indicates an energy meter is present (treat as a boolean meter_present=true).

Exclusions

Exclude high street shops.

Exclude chain establishments (use group/brand ownership from Emailmovers data; also exclude if location_count >= 5 or brand is in a known UK chains list returned by Emailmovers API).

Geography

UK-wide by default; add optional region filter (England/Scotland/Wales/Northern Ireland) and county filter.

Company quality gates

Must have: company name, at least one contact person (first/last name), direct email and/or switchboard, and a validated address.

De-duplicate by company_id and address_postcode; suppress repeats for 30 days.

Scoring & ordering

Score leads and order the daily email by:

Manufacturing (top),

Industrial units & garage services,

Hotels & guest houses,

Construction with meter_present=true.

Within each bucket, sort by most recent move-in date, then by company size (headcount) descending.

Data source

All B2B data must come from the Emailmovers API.

Endpoints to call (use realistic placeholders that will be swapped to production):

GET /recent-movers?from_date=YYYY-MM-DD&to_date=YYYY-MM-DD&country=UK&premises_type=...&sic_codes=...

GET /company/{company_id} for enrichment (contacts, emails, phones, group ownership, meter flag).

GET /chains/list (or equivalent) to identify chain brands.

Include API key management (env var), rate limiting, retries with exponential backoff, and request/response logging (PII redacted in logs).

Outputs

Daily email alert (default send time 08:00 Europe/London) containing up to 50 leads/day (configurable), with CSV attachment and inline preview of the top 10.

Fields: company name, move-in date, premises type, sector/SIC, headcount band, contact person (role), phone, email, full address, website, meter_present (Y/N), chain_flag (Y/N), data source = Emailmovers.

Web dashboard

Search and filter panel mirroring server-side filters.

Lead list with badges (Manufacturing, Industrial, Garage, Hotel/Guest House, Construction+Meter).

Detail drawer for each lead with full enrichment from Emailmovers.

Export to CSV and copy-to-clipboard for selected rows.

Suppression controls (hide a company for N days, mark as contacted, do-not-contact).

Audit trail showing when the lead was first seen and when notifications were sent.

Pricing model (configurable, not enforced)

Add an Admin > Pricing page with editable tiers based on lead volume and freshness:

Tier A: Daily feed (0–50/day)

Tier B: Daily feed (51–150/day)

Tier C: Near-real-time (hourly digest)

Display the active tier and current month-to-date lead count in the dashboard header.

Email delivery

Use a transactional email provider (e.g., SendGrid/SES) with:

DKIM/SPF ready config fields.

Per-recipient unsubscribe link (dashboard flag do_not_email=true).

Fallback to digest-only if no qualifying leads for the day.

Provide a simple email template:

Subject: Daily Movers: {{date}} — {{lead_count}} Manufacturing-Focused Opportunities

Preheader: Fresh movers filtered to your criteria (manufacturing first).

Body sections: Top 10 table, “View all” link to dashboard, CSV attached.

Compliance & governance

Respect GDPR/UK privacy rules; only use business contact data from Emailmovers.

Store minimal PII, encrypt at rest, and provide a global opt-out list.

Include a Data Source label: “Powered by Emailmovers”.

Tech notes

Modern, responsive UI (React + Tailwind) with server-side API layer.

Timezone: Europe/London everywhere.

CRON/scheduler for daily job at 08:00 UK time, with manual “Run now” button.

Error states: graceful degradation with “No new movers today” message.

Unit tests for filter logic (inclusion/exclusion and chain detection).

Config via .env (EMAILMOVERS_API_KEY, EMAIL_PROVIDER_KEY, CRON_SCHEDULE).

Acceptance criteria

Able to trigger a fetch from Emailmovers API, apply filters/exclusions, de-duplicate, score, and render a dashboard list.

Sends a daily email at 08:00 UK time with at least one valid lead when available, including CSV attachment and “View all” link.

Demonstrates suppression, opt-out, and chain exclusion working as intended.

All company and contact data in the prototype is obtained via the Emailmovers API (no other data sources).
